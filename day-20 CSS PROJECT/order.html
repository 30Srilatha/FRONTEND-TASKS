Delivery & Pickup

Enjoy the convenience of fresh desserts delivered to your doorstep or ready for pickup at Sweet Haven.

We carefully prepare every order and deliver it fresh on your selected date and time whenever possible. You'll receive a confirmation once your order is ready for delivery.

Prefer to pick up your order? Simply choose the pickup option during checkout, and your freshly baked treats will be ready for collection at the scheduled time.

Special Requests

Have a unique request or a personal touch in mind? Let us know! Whether it's a custom cake message, specific decoration, dietary preference, allergy information, or special delivery instructions, we'll do our best to accommodate your needs and make your order truly memorable.

Note: Please include your requests during checkout. While we'll make every effort to fulfill them, availability may vary depending on the order and preparation time.
